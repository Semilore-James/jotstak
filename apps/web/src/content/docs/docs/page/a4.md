The page is not a print setting bolted on at the end; it is the frame everything is measured against. Explain the model once, here, so the width and printing pages can just refer to it.

## What this page has to cover

- A4 at 96dpi: the sheet, the printable width, the text column, the margin channel
- 36 rows on a portrait page, 24 on a landscape one
- The screen is continuous; only print cuts pages
- What fits on screen fits on paper — why the preview is the same width as the sheet
- Changing the paper (when `@page` grows to accept it)
